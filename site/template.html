Prices include VAT and exclude shipping. Each row is the cheapest in-stock flavour; with “Every size” off, each product shows its best-value size. “All shops” includes EU shops that ship to Spain and France (Body & Fit, ESN); their checkout price can differ slightly.

€ / kg protein divides the price per kg by the protein per 100 g read from the shop’s nutrition label. A blank means the label wasn’t readable. Creatine € / month assumes 3 g a day.
